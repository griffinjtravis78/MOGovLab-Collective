Independent Research Initiative: The MOGovLab Collective is an independent, non-partisan public-sector research initiative operating during a formal development phase. Participation as a volunteer does not constitute employment, legal representation, or an official agency appointment with the State of Missouri or any government entity.

Data Privacy & Confidentiality: Information collected in this intake survey is used exclusively for internal research coordination, skill-matching, and communications. We respect volunteer privacy and will not sell, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to external parties.

Compliance with Public Standards: All research and investigations conducted under the collective's pillars rely strictly on lawfully obtained public records, open data architectures, and transparent methodologies.
