Tax-deductible crowdfunding dedicated to statutory records acquisition, secure digital infrastructure, and open-access research.

Model C Fiscal Sponsorship Funding Pipeline

To ensure absolute financial transparency and tax-deductible giving without heavy administrative overhead, contributions to the MOGovLab Collective are processed via our established 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor partner.

Funds are held in a restricted account explicitly earmarked for our initiatives and disbursed strictly for approved project expenses, such as:

  • Missouri Sunshine Law request and duplication fees
  • Secure digital archiving and repository management
  • Open-access academic publication costs
