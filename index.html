MOGovLab Collective

Data-Driven Civic Research & State Government Transparency

Our Mission & Purpose

The MOGovLab Collective is an independent, non-partisan public-sector research initiative dedicated to modernizing government operations, increasing procurement equity, and evaluating state administrative effectiveness in Missouri. We leverage open data infrastructure, rigorous methodology, and permanent public archiving to empower policymakers, journalists, researchers, and citizens with actionable insights.

Notice: The MOGovLab Collective is currently in the process of formalizing its legal and administrative structure. Core infrastructure, secure routing, and our public research pipelines are fully active.

Our Process & Core Pillars

1. MOVERS Project

Tracking, analyzing, and mapping state workforce data and administrative movements to evaluate institutional effectiveness and transparency.

2. WBE Score

Evaluating and benchmarking Women-Owned Business Enterprise participation, equity metrics, and contracting accountability across public systems.

3. Procurement Gap

Conducting empirical analysis on public sector purchasing and vendor distribution to identify systemic gaps and foster open competition.
