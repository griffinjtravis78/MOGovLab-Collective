MOGovLab Collective

"From public records to public progress."

Our Mission & Purpose

The MOGovLab Collective is an independent, non-partisan public-sector research initiative dedicated to modernizing government operations, increasing procurement equity, and evaluating state administrative effectiveness in Missouri. We leverage open data infrastructure, rigorous methodology, and permanent public archiving to empower policymakers, journalists, researchers, and citizens with actionable insights.

Status: Currently in active development, establishing our legal, structural, and technical infrastructure ahead of full research deployment.

Our Process & Core Pillars

1. MOWatch

Systemic Surveillance & Oversight: Monitoring state administrative shifts, public records, and institutional behaviors to maintain continuous transparency and accountability across government agencies.

2. MOData

Open Data Architecture: Gathering, cleaning, and structuring empirical public datasets—such as workforce metrics and procurement logs—and permanently archiving them via Harvard Dataverse with verified DOIs.

3. MOSystems

Technical Infrastructure: Building open-source software, secure web applications, and reproducible codebases (hosted on GitHub and Cloudflare) to ensure civic tech tools are robust, secure, and accessible.

4. MOActions

Empirical Impact & Policy Execution: Translating raw data analysis into actionable policy briefs, structural recommendations, and public transparency initiatives that foster open competition and equitable governance.
