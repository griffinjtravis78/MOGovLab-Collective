Our Collectives & Initiatives

Active research initiatives driving empirical analysis and public administration reform in Missouri.

1. MOVERS Project

Focused on tracking, analyzing, and documenting state administrative mobility, personnel trends, and institutional workforce dynamics across Missouri public agencies.

2. WBE Score

Evaluating and scoring state contracting equity, vendor diversity metrics, and Women-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE) participation rates through transparent data synthesis.

3. Procurement Gap Analysis

Investigating systemic IT and administrative procurement bottlenecks, vendor capture risks, and contractual discrepancies within Missouri public sector purchasing.
