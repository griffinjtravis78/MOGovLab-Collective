Our Collectives & Initiatives

The operational pillars driving our public administration research and reform.

1. MO-Watch (Identification & Discovery)

Focused on uncovering and documenting systemic governance failures, transparency gaps, and administrative bottlenecks across Missouri public institutions using statutory Missouri Sunshine Law requests and primary records.

2. MO-Data (Synthesis & Analysis)

Processing empirical evidence, mapping operational workflows using Python and Tableau dashboards, and structuring peer-reviewed case studies for academic repositories like Zenodo and Harvard Dataverse.

3. MO-Systems (Solution Design & Architecture)

Developing robust administrative blueprints, policy frameworks, and training models (applying ADDIE and Kirkpatrick evaluation frameworks) to modernize public sector workflows.

4. MO-Action (Implementation & Execution)

Translating research into real-world Missouri impact through policy briefs, educational media (such as the State HR Modernization podcast series), legislative recommendations, and public education.
